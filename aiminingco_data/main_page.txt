=== ABOUT STABLE DIFFUSION & IMAGE GENERATION ===

Image generation with diffusion models is one of the most accessible high-end AI workloads.
- VRAM floor depends on model:
  - Stable Diffusion 1.5: 6 GB
  - SDXL: 8-12 GB
  - FLUX.1 [dev] in full FP16: 24 GB
- Quantized variants (FP8, NF4) cut requirements roughly in half
- Dominant frontends: ComfyUI, Automatic1111
- Training/fine-tuning (DreamBooth, LoRA): 24 GB+

Key hardware considerations:
- VRAM is the hard floor — undersized GPUs cannot run larger models
- FP8/NF4 quantization opens up FLUX-class models on consumer cards
- ComfyUI offers fine-grained memory management for tight VRAM budgets
- Batch throughput scales with GPU count for commercial workflows
